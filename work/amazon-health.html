Health for the Amazon

Three telemedicine apps that bring diagnosis and follow-up to communities far from any hospital.

RoleLead Designer, PO
TeamSenior designer, medical experts, engineers
TimelineAbout a year, to Sep 2026
Products3 apps
StatusCase in progress
Case in progress. The full write-up, with screens, is being prepared. Below is the outline of the work.

01Overview

Three telemedicine apps for remote communities in the Brazilian Amazon, where a doctor or a lab can be days away by boat. The apps support chronic care follow-up and the diagnosis of tuberculosis and malaria, including AI-assisted image analysis of lab samples.

I worked as lead designer and product owner for about a year, with a senior designer assisting, alongside medical experts and engineers.

02How the work ran

  • Weekly delivery cycles, each closed with an executive review
  • A running backlog of fixes and requests from the client's managers, organised per app
  • Clinical accuracy checked with the medical team, for example on the reliability of image analysis
  • One shared design language across three different products

03Coming next

The full case will cover the field context, the diagnosis flows, how AI results are presented to health workers, and the design system behind the three apps.
